# 🧱 Habit Stacking Worksheet

## Building New Habits by Linking to Existing Ones

Habit stacking uses the formula: **"After I [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT]."**

This technique works because:
- Your brain already has neural pathways for existing habits
- Linking new behaviors to old ones makes them easier to remember
- The existing habit becomes a trigger for the new one

## Habit Stacking Examples

### Morning Stacks
- After I pour my morning coffee, I will write down 3 things I'm grateful for.
- After I brush my teeth, I will do 5 squats.
- After I sit down at my desk, I will write my most important task for the day.
- After I wake up and turn off my alarm, I will make my bed.

### Mealtime Stacks
- After I sit down for dinner, I will share one good thing from my day.
- After I finish eating, I will drink a full glass of water.
- After I load the dishwasher, I will wipe down the counters.
- After I pack my lunch, I will set out clothes for tomorrow.

### Evening Stacks
- After I put on my pajamas, I will lay out tomorrow's clothes.
- After I get in bed, I will read one page.
- After I set my alarm, I will take 3 deep breaths.
- After dinner, I will take a 5-minute walk outside.

### Weekly Stacks
- After Sunday dinner, I will plan meals for the week.
- After I pay bills, I will review our budget.
- After Saturday morning cartoons, we will do a 10-minute family clean-up.

## Troubleshooting Habit Stacks

### "I keep forgetting"

**Solutions:**
- Add a visual cue (post-it note, object placement)
- Set a phone reminder until it becomes automatic
- Make the new habit even smaller

### "The timing doesn't work"

**Solutions:**
- Try a different anchor habit
- Adjust the time of day
- Shorten the new habit further

### "I don't have time"

**Solutions:**
- Make the new habit take less than 1 minute
- Stack onto a habit that has natural waiting time
- Combine with something you're already doing (audiobook while commuting)

### "I start strong but fade"

**Solutions:**
- Celebrate each success (even tiny acknowledgment)
- Track visibly where you'll see it
- Don't add more stacks until current one is solid

## Advanced: Building a Habit Chain

Once individual stacks are solid, you can link them into chains:

**Example Morning Chain:**
1. After I wake up → I make my bed (30 seconds)
2. After I make my bed → I drink a glass of water (30 seconds)
3. After I drink water → I do 5 stretches (1 minute)
4. After I stretch → I write 3 gratitudes (1 minute)
