# 🎉 Celebration Log

## Recognizing and Celebrating Family Achievements

Celebrating progress—no matter how small—is essential to the Kaizen journey. This log helps you capture, remember, and honor your family's improvements.

---

## Why Celebration Matters

- **Reinforces positive behavior** - What gets celebrated gets repeated
- **Builds confidence** - "We CAN improve!"
- **Creates positive memories** - Associates growth with good feelings
- **Maintains motivation** - Fuel for the journey
- **Strengthens family bonds** - Shared joy connects us

## The Win Jar System

### How It Works:
1. Keep a jar and slips of paper visible
2. When anyone has a win, write it down and add to jar
3. Read wins at weekly family meetings
4. Review all wins at the end of each month

### Win Jar Prompts:
- "I improved at..."
- "I tried something new..."
- "I helped someone by..."
- "I kept going even when..."
- "I learned..."
- "Our family got better at..."
