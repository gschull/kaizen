📚 Learn kaiZEN

Understanding the philosophy of continuous improvement

🎋 What is kaiZEN?

kaiZEN (改善) is a Japanese philosophy meaning "change for better" or "continuous improvement."

The word combines two concepts:

  • 改 (kai) = change
  • 善 (zen) = good, better

The core idea is simple: small, daily improvements compound into significant results over time.

If you get 1% better each day for one year, you'll be 37 times better by year's end!

1.01^365 = 37.78

🌟 The 5 Core Principles

🐢

Small Steps

Focus on tiny, manageable improvements rather than overwhelming changes

👥

Everyone Participates

If you're in a group, everyone contributes. In personal mode, it's just you.

🚫

No Blame

Focus on solutions and systems, not fault-finding

📚

Continuous Learning

Embrace mistakes as opportunities to grow

🎉

Celebrate Progress

Acknowledge all improvements, no matter how small

🔑 The Magic Question

"What small step can I take right now to improve?"

Ask this question:

  • When facing a problem
  • When feeling stuck
  • When wanting to grow
  • Before starting any task

⚡ kaiZEN vs. Innovation

💡 Quick Tips

  • Start smaller than you think necessary
  • Focus on ONE improvement at a time
  • Celebrate every win, no matter how small
  • Mistakes are learning opportunities
  • Consistency beats intensity
  • Make good habits easy, bad habits hard
  • Track your progress visually
  • Review and adjust regularly

📖 The 5S Framework

5S is an organization method that creates efficiency through organization. Applied to daily life, it helps reduce friction and stress.

🗂️ 1. Seiri (整理) - Sort

▼

Meaning: Separate the necessary from the unnecessary

Questions to Ask:

  • "Have I used this in the last year?"
  • "Does this bring value or joy to my life?"
  • "If I didn't have this, would I miss it?"

📍 2. Seiton (整頓) - Set in Order

▼

Meaning: A place for everything, everything in its place

Key Concept: Items should be stored where they're used, not where there's space.

✨ 3. Seiso (清掃) - Shine

▼

Meaning: Keep things clean and inspect through cleaning

Beyond Physical: Also applies to relationships - keep communication clean and clear.

📋 4. Seiketsu (清潔) - Standardize

▼

Meaning: Create consistent practices

Key Concept: Routines reduce decision fatigue and create predictability.

🔄 5. Shitsuke (躾) - Sustain

▼

Meaning: Maintain and review standards

Key Concept: The hardest part is maintaining. Build in regular check-ins.

❓ Help

Full setup instructions and support.

Getting Started

  1. Open Settings → Account and enter your name.
  2. If you’re sharing a device, use Switch user to change names anytime.
  3. If you want to sync with others, go to Group and Join/Create a group.

Installing the App (PWA)

  • iPhone/iPad (Safari): Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android (Chrome): Menu → “Install app”.
  • If the install button appears in the menu, tap it and follow prompts.

Using Goals & Habits

  • Goals: Add a goal, track progress, and mark complete by reaching 100%.
  • Goal History: Completed goals move to History where you can reopen or edit them.
  • Habits: Add a habit and check it off daily. Streaks are tracked automatically.
  • Habit History: Archived habits stay in History and can be restored.

Troubleshooting

  • If buttons feel unresponsive, fully close the app and reopen (PWA), or refresh in the browser.
  • If you see “permission denied”, the Firestore rules may need updating.
  • If “Still loading…” persists, disable blockers/VPN and check your connection.
