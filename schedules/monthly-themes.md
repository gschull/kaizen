# 📆 Monthly Kaizen Themes

## A Year of Family Improvement Focus Areas

This guide provides suggested monthly themes to keep your family Kaizen journey fresh and comprehensive throughout the year.

---

## How to Use Monthly Themes

1. **Choose a theme** at the start of each month (or use these suggestions)
2. **Focus your family meetings** on that theme
3. **Select small improvements** related to the theme
4. **Track progress** throughout the month
5. **Review and celebrate** at month's end

## January: Fresh Starts 🎯

**Theme:** Goals & Intentions

**Core Question:** "What do we want to improve this year?"

### Focus Areas:
- Setting meaningful family and individual goals
- Creating vision boards or goal statements
- Establishing the Kaizen habit
- Starting fresh with positive energy

### Suggested Small Improvements:
- [ ] Create a family vision/goals board
- [ ] Each person sets one personal goal
- [ ] Establish weekly meeting rhythm
- [ ] Start a gratitude practice

### Weekly Focus:
| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Reflect on last year, dream about this year |
| 2 | Set specific, small family goal |
| 3 | Set individual goals |
| 4 | Create tracking systems |

### Family Activity:
Create a family vision board together—magazines, markers, poster board. What does your ideal year look like?

### Discussion Questions:
- What are we proud of from last year?
- What do we want more of this year?
- What's one thing each of us wants to learn or improve?

### End of Month Check:
- [ ] Family goal established
- [ ] Individual goals set
- [ ] Tracking system in place
- [ ] First month of meetings completed

### Focus Areas:

|------|-------|

## March: Organize 🧹

**Theme:** Home & Systems

**Core Question:** "What systems can we improve?"

### Focus Areas:
- Home organization
- Decluttering
- Creating better systems
- Reducing daily friction
- Spring cleaning

### Suggested Small Improvements:
- [ ] One drawer/area organized per week
- [ ] Morning launch pad created
- [ ] Paper/mail system established
- [ ] 10-minute daily tidy routine

### Weekly Focus:
| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Identify biggest organization pain point |
| 2 | Create one new system |
| 3 | Declutter one area per person |
| 4 | Establish maintenance routines |

### Family Activity:
"Family Clean Sweep" - Pick one room. Everyone has 15 minutes to organize their assigned area. Celebrate with a treat!

### Discussion Questions:
- What area of our home causes the most frustration?
- What would make mornings/evenings smoother?
- What can we get rid of that we don't need?

### End of Month Check:
- [ ] Major pain point addressed
- [ ] New system implemented
- [ ] Decluttering completed
- [ ] Maintenance routine established
