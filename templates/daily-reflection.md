## Themed Reflection Prompts

### For Personal Growth

- What new thing did I learn today?
- When did I step outside my comfort zone?
- What skill did I practice today?
- What would my future self thank me for doing today?

### For Relationships

- How did I show kindness today?
- Who did I connect with today?
- What could I do to be a better [friend/sibling/child/parent]?
- Who needs my attention or support right now?

### For Productivity

- What was my most important accomplishment today?
- What task did I avoid, and why?
- What took longer than expected?
- What could I systematize or simplify?

### For Mindset

- What negative thought did I have today? Is it true?
- When did I feel most confident today?
- What assumption did I question today?
- What would I do if I weren't afraid?

### For Health & Wellness

- How did I move my body today?
- What did I eat that made me feel good?
- How many hours did I sleep last night?
- What did I do to relax today?

## Family Dinner Reflection

*Quick prompts for family mealtime conversation:*

### Round-Robin Questions

Take turns answering one of these:

1. "What made you smile today?"
2. "What's something you learned today?"
3. "Who did something kind for you today?"
4. "What's something you're looking forward to?"
5. "What's one word to describe your day?"
6. "What was the hardest part of your day?"
7. "What are you grateful for right now?"
8. "What's something that went better than expected?"

### High-Low-Buffalo

Each person shares:
- **High:** Best part of the day
- **Low:** Hardest part of the day
- **Buffalo:** Something random/interesting that happened

## Reflection Tips

### When to Reflect

- **Morning:** Set intentions for the day
- **Midday:** Quick energy/focus check
- **Evening:** Review and gratitude
- **Weekly:** Deeper review and planning

### How to Build the Habit

1. **Same time each day** - Link to existing habit (after brushing teeth, before bed)
2. **Same place** - Keep journal in a consistent spot
3. **Start tiny** - Just one question at first
4. **No perfection** - Bullet points are fine
5. **Forgive misses** - Just start again tomorrow

### What to Do with Reflections

- Review weekly for patterns
- Celebrate progress monthly
- Adjust goals based on insights
- Share relevant reflections at family meetings
