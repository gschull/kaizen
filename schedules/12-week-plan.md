# 📅 12-Week Family Kaizen Implementation Plan

## Your Structured Rollout Guide

This 12-week plan provides a systematic approach to implementing Kaizen in your family. Each week builds on the previous one, gradually introducing concepts and practices.

---

## Overview

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Foundation | 1-3 | Introduction and first improvement |
| Building | 4-6 | Establishing routines and habits |
| Expanding | 7-9 | Adding depth and individual goals |
| Sustaining | 10-12 | Solidifying and celebrating |

---
