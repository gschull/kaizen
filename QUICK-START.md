## What is Kaizen?

**Kaizen (改善)** = "Change for Better"

A Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement through small, daily steps.

---

## The Core Idea

> **Get 1% better every day.**
> Small steps → Big results over time.

---

## 5 kaiZEN Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 🐢 **Small Steps** | Tiny improvements beat big plans |
| 👥 **Everyone Participates** | In a group, every voice matters |
| 🚫 **No Blame** | Focus on solutions, not fault |
| 📚 **Learn from Mistakes** | Failures are teachers |
| 🎉 **Celebrate Progress** | Every win counts |

---

## The Magic Question

> **"What small step can I take right now to improve?"**

Ask this when:
- Facing a problem
- Feeling stuck
- Wanting to grow
- During weekly reviews (solo or group)

---

## Quick Start Steps

### Week 1
1. ☐ Pick one area to improve
2. ☐ Choose ONE area to improve
3. ☐ Pick the SMALLEST possible step
4. ☐ Start tracking

### Ongoing
5. ☐ Do a weekly 15-min review
6. ☐ Celebrate all wins
7. ☐ Add new focus when ready

---

## Weekly Review (15 min)

1. **Celebrate** (3 min) - What went well?
2. **Reflect** (3 min) - What did we learn?
3. **Discuss** (4 min) - Any challenges?
4. **Plan** (3 min) - What's next?
5. **Appreciate** (2 min) - Note one thing you're grateful for

---

## Daily Touchpoints

| Time | Question |
|------|----------|
| **Morning** | "What's one thing you'll improve today?" |
| **Dinner** | "What went well today?" |
| **Bedtime** | "What did you learn today?" |

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Starting too big  
❌ Changing too many things at once  
❌ Forgetting to celebrate  
❌ Giving up after one miss  
❌ Making it feel like a chore

✅ **Smaller is better**
