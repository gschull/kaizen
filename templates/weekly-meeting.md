## Quick Reference: Meeting Roles

**Facilitator:** Keeps meeting on track, asks questions
**Timekeeper:** Watches the clock, gives warnings
**Note-taker:** Records decisions and commitments
**Celebration Leader:** Leads appreciations and celebrations

*(Rotate roles weekly to build everyone's skills)*

---

## Alternative: 5-Minute Mini Meeting

For busy weeks or families just starting:

1. **Wins** (1 min): One highlight from each person
2. **Focus** (2 min): What's one small thing we're working on?
3. **Gratitude** (2 min): One appreciation each

---

## Alternative: Car Meeting

For families always on the go:

- Do the meeting during a regular car trip
- Same agenda, adjusted for driving
- Driver is facilitator
- Someone else takes notes on phone

---

## Meeting Don'ts

❌ Don't let it become a lecture  
❌ Don't skip celebrations  
❌ Don't allow blame or criticism  
❌ Don't try to solve everything at once  
❌ Don't force participation  
❌ Don't go over time  

---

## Meeting Do's

✅ Do keep it positive  
✅ Do give everyone a voice  
✅ Do celebrate all progress  
✅ Do focus on ONE improvement  
✅ Do end with appreciation  
✅ Do follow through on commitments  

---

## Post-Meeting
